California Law

Probate fees are based on the gross value of the estate.

Not the size of the mistake.
Not just the $500 account.

If ANY significant asset sits outside your trust — real estate, accounts, property — probate is on the table.

Probate in California means months of court process, public records, and fees that can reach 4–6% of your gross estate.
